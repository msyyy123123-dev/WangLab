About Us

We are affiliated with the College of Plant Protection, China Agricultural University.

Our research group primarily focuses on the formation and evolutionary mechanisms of insect diversity and adaptability. Currently, the laboratory investigates the molecular mechanisms of phenotypic development in Kallima butterflies, the evolutionary genomics and phylogenetics of Saturniidae, and actively promotes the interdisciplinary application of traditional agricultural plant pathology and intelligent diagnostics (e.g., intelligent screening of wheat diseases).
